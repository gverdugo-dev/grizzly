Reading (2026-06-11):
- JSON read at scale is grizzly's strongest result: 10M rows in 4.15s vs
  polars 46.7s (11x) and pandas 234s (56x). Best JSON loader on the table
  at every size >= 10k.
- Tiny inputs (1-1k rows): grizzly totals are µs-scale; both Python engines
  pay ms of fixed per-operation overhead.
- CSV read at scale belongs to polars (SIMD + Arrow): 320ms vs 4.42s at 10M.
  Known and documented as out of scope.
- Sort is grizzly's clearest ceiling: single-threaded permutation sort loses
  to BOTH engines from ~100k rows (10M: 7.0s vs pandas 2.24s, polars 0.70s).
  Candidate for the next performance milestone.
- GroupBy: beats pandas at every size; competitive with polars to ~100k,
  behind at 1M+ (polars parallelizes).
- Writers: beat pandas everywhere (CSV 1M: 0.67s vs 5.22s); polars faster
  from ~100k.
